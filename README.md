## 💡 Tips for Success

### 1. Strategic Alignment
- Align your project with the most common SDGs
- Match your mission with corporate priorities
- Demonstrate measurable impact

### 2. Build Partnerships
- Collaborate with international organizations
- Work with local universities
- Partner with local government
- Join civil society networks

### 3. Professional Communication
- Attend CSR events
- Network with corporate sustainability officers
- Participate in conferences and workshops
- Use social media professionally

### 4. Show Impact
- Use concrete numbers
- Document success stories
- Collect beneficiary testimonials
- Use photos and videos

### 5. Long-term Relationships
- Position your NGO as a strategic partner
- Don't just ask for one-time funding
- Show how you can help companies achieve sustainability goals

## 📈 Success Metrics

### Short-term (3 months):
- Submit 5+ funding applications
- Build relationships with 3+ companies
- Attend 2+ CSR events

### Medium-term (6 months):
- Secure at least one funding source
- Establish partnership with international organization
- Update impact assessment system

### Long-term (12 months):
- Secure 2-3 funding sources
- Build strong reputation in the field
- Expand project scope
